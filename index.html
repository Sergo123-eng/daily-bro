A nudge toward a fuller day.

Daily Bro helps you make space for movement, people, and rest. Gym Bro gives you a manageable movement session and permission to recover.

Try it: choose your energy and available time, build a plan, then make a step easier, swap it, add it to your calendar, or mark it done.

Guided mode selects written suggestions using your check-in. It detects common wellbeing requests and limits, but may miss nuanced prompts. It is not a live AI model. This portfolio demo does not use a live language model or send messages to an AI service.

Club suggestions are ideas to explore, not verified local events. Calendar files create personal reminders; they do not register you for an event.

General wellbeing ideas, not medical care or a personalized training prescription. Choose comfortable movement and stop if it hurts.

Your day stays yours.

Your plan and completed actions are stored in this browser. They do not sync across devices. Your free-text check-in and conversation are kept only in this tab's memory.

Your messages are processed by the guided demo in this browser. They are not sent to an AI service. No analytics are included. The host and font provider may receive ordinary network requests.
